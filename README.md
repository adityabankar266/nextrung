# NextRung

Career guidance from people one rung ahead.

NextRung is a two-sided career platform concept. Learners, from final-year students to experienced professionals, book one-to-one sessions with verified working professionals in their field. Professionals enroll as guides, set their own services and prices, and run sessions through their own portal.

## What the concept site shows

- **Services** for freshers (question bank, workshops, mock panels, resume review) and experienced people (career switch roadmaps, promotion and leadership coaching, senior interview prep, offer discussions)
- **Fields**: Software Development, Business Analysis, Quality Assurance, Data Analytics, Marketing and Leadership, each with sample questions and a case brief
- **Guide directory** filtered by field
- **Two portals**: a learner dashboard and a professional (guide) dashboard
- **Two enrollment paths**: "I want guidance" and "I want to guide"
- **Pricing** for learners and the platform fee for guides
- **Campus and company programs**

## Status

Static concept preview. Prices, the platform fee and guide profiles are placeholders, and the forms do not send data yet.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step is needed.

Questions

Before you join

Who is this platform for?

Students, recent graduates and working professionals who want help with interviews, a career switch, a promotion or their resume. Experienced professionals can also join as guides.

Are sessions online?

Yes. All one-to-one sessions run on video. Campus and company workshops can also be held in person.

How is this different from AI mock interview apps?

AI apps are useful for unlimited practice. Here, an experienced person questions you live, reacts to your answers and gives you advice based on real hiring decisions.

How are guides verified?

Each applicant has a short call with our team. We check their experience, their role and that they have interviewed or led people in their field.

How do guides get paid?

Guides set their own prices. Learners pay through the platform, and guides receive their share every week after the platform fee.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. We help you prepare and show you exactly where you stand. The offer depends on you and the employer.
